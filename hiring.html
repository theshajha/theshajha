The Problem I Solve

  • For startups: You're spending weeks interviewing candidates who look great on paper but don't fit your culture. By the time you realize it, you've lost momentum.
  • For talent: You're tired of recruiters who don't understand what you actually do. You want to join a rocket ship, not just any company with funding.
  • The gap: Recruiters focus on keywords and years of experience. The real match (values, ambition, working style) comes too late in the process.

What I Offer

Access to a curated network of senior talent (5+ years). I only introduce candidates I'd hire myself. No bulk resumes, just people who fit.

For Talent

My Approach

  • Senior roles only. I work with experienced professionals (5+ years). No entry-level or bulk hiring.

  • Founder perspective. I've built and scaled teams. I know what works and what doesn't.
